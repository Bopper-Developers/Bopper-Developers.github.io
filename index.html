Bopper Finance

Keep card benefits in view.

Bopper Finance is a private household app for tracking card benefits, usage, and renewal dates on iPhone and Mac.

Google Calendar connection

Each household member can choose to connect their own Google account from Profile → Calendar. Bopper Finance creates a separate calendar in that account and adds an all-day event for each unused benefit with a tracked deadline. Marking a benefit fully used removes its event. Events do not create Calendar alerts.

The connection asks only for access to secondary calendars created by Bopper Finance. It does not ask for access to your existing calendars.

Your choice

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. You can disconnect from Profile at any time. Bopper Finance removes its tracked benefit events and revokes its Google access when disconnection succeeds.
